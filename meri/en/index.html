Solutions

Intelligent systems for the routine work of shipping.

Arrival planning

Predicts when each ship will reach its berth, and recommends the speed that gets it there on time instead of early.

  • ETA from AIS, weather and port data
  • Just-in-time speed advice
  • Fuel and emissions saved, per voyage
Fleet knowledge

An assistant that answers from your own manuals, procedures, charter parties and voyage history.

  • Works on board without a connection
  • Points to the source document
  • Ashore and at sea
Cargo documents

Bills of lading, invoices, customs declarations and certificates are read, checked and filed.

  • Fields read straight into your systems
  • Mismatches flagged for review
  • A searchable archive
Emissions reporting

Fuel and emissions data gathered from voyage records into the reports EU ETS, FuelEU Maritime and MRV require.

  • Fuel and CO₂ for every voyage
  • Report drafts ready for verification
  • Missing data caught early
Agentic workflows for leadership

Agents gather figures from your systems, prepare reports and meeting material, follow up on open items and draft replies. Leadership decides; the agents do the groundwork.

  • A weekly situation report from your own systems
  • Meeting material and replies drafted
  • Every step logged and approved by a person

Your infrastructure

Your data stays where it is.

Shipping data is commercially sensitive, and ships are often on a thin connection. We build on open models and run them where you decide.

  • On board and ashore. Runs on the vessel or in your own server room, and keeps working on a weak satellite link.
  • Confidential. Rates, customers and routes do not pass through anyone else's cloud.
  • Fits your systems. Results arrive through APIs, dashboards or the tools your team already uses.
