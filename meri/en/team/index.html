The team

The people behind Enlightened Bits.

We're two engineers from Helsinki, both Aalto University graduates. We build intelligent systems for shipping companies, ports and freight forwarders, running on infrastructure our customers control.

How we got here

We met at Aalto University and kept coming back to the same idea: Finland has extreme talent density, and it can, should and will be a leader in intelligent systems. Finnish organisations shouldn't have to hand their most important work to someone else's cloud.

We started out building intelligent systems on organisations' own servers. In marine logistics that matters more than almost anywhere: the data is confidential, ships are often on a thin connection, and every hour waiting in port costs money. So that is where we now focus.
