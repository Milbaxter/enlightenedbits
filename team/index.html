How we got here

We met at Aalto University and kept coming back to the same idea: European organisations shouldn't have to hand their most important work to an American cloud or an American AI company. For work that is regulated, private, or simply important, you should be able to run it on European infrastructure, on open-source models, and keep your data, your costs and your freedom in your own hands. Enlightened Bits is how we make that practical for organisations across Europe.
