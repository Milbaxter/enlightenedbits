How we got here

We met at Aalto University and kept coming back to the same conviction: the most capable AI shouldn't only live in someone else's data centre. For work that's regulated, private, or simply important, you should be able to run it on your own machines — keeping your data, your costs, and your freedom in your own hands. Enlightened Bits is how we make that practical for organisations across Europe.
