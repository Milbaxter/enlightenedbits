Emergent intelligence · Open-source bleeding edge · Finland & Europe's race to AGI

Our explicit mission is to become the #1 company in Finland and Europe working to create Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) — not by burning billions on brute-force foundational model pretraining, but by pioneering the real frontier: harness architecture, context engineering, and emergent modular intelligence.

Operational Definition

What is AGI?

AGI is a competent agent that can solve any problem in digital space that a human might be able to.

If a skilled human sitting at a workstation with a terminal, browser, editor, and tools can solve a problem, an AGI agent must be able to reason through it, plan it, execute it, verify it, and self-correct until the goal is achieved.

Paradigm Shift

A different kind of race

The thesis is that you can take part in the race for AGI without being a foundation model lab with massive amounts of compute, people, and capital.

Why harness engineering wins:

01

Emergent Intelligence

Smarter intelligence emerges via modular plugins. As you add tool ecosystems, sandboxes, and verification loops, general problem-solving capability scales exponentially without retraining base model weights.

02

DeepSeek Open Bleeding Edge

We build directly on the cutting edge of open-source agent harnesses, starting with DeepSeek's open-source harness architecture. Open models allow complete inspectability and rapid experimentation.

03

Context Engineering

True reasoning is dynamic context management: self-correcting execution trajectories, episodic memory, hierarchical delegation, and grounded environment feedback.

04

European Leadership

Finland's extreme talent density in systems engineering makes it a natural home for AGI research. Europe can lead through agility, open source, and architectural clarity.
