Does it pay for itself?

Cloud AI is a meter that never stops. You pay for every token, for as long as you use it. A local machine is a one-time cost, and after that running your models costs little more than electricity, a few euros a month. The more you use AI, the faster it pays for itself. Here is the rough math, anchored on what you already spend on cloud APIs:

Your monthly cloud AI spendPays back a local box inYou save per year after
€500 / month~5 months~€5,900
€1,000 / month~2.5 months~€11,900
€2,000 / month~6 weeks~€23,900

Assumes a one-time hardware cost of a few thousand euros plus a few euros a month for electricity, and that a local model fits the work — the savings are strongest for steady, repeated workloads. Over the 3 to 5 year life of the machine that runs into tens of thousands of euros saved, and your data never leaves the building.

There is even a small upside when it sits idle. When you are not using the box, you can rent out its spare capacity, for example on Vast.ai, turning an idle machine into a small earner.

Why local AI

For regulated and privacy-sensitive work like law, finance, healthcare and the public sector, sending data to a foreign cloud is often a non-starter. Local AI gives you the productivity of modern models without the exposure. Your information never leaves your control, you are not locked into a provider's pricing, and there is no black box you depend on.

And if you simply value your freedom: local AI is yours. You get full ownership of the hardware, the models and the data. You get real privacy, because nothing leaves your control. And you get the most cost-efficient AI you can run, because there is no meter and no middleman. Your AI answers to you, not to a provider's terms of service, rate limits or content rules.
