Does it pay for itself?

Cloud AI is a meter that never stops. You pay for every token, forever. A local box is a one-time cost, and after that the inference is basically free. Here is the rough math, based on the cloud API spend you would replace:

Cloud API spend you replace≈ tokens / monthPays back the $5,000 in
$500 / month~125M~10 months
$1,000 / month~250M~5 months
$2,000 / month~500M~2.5 months

Assumes a typical cloud rate of about $4 per million tokens. After payback, you keep running the same work for the 3 to 5 year life of the machine, for the price of electricity (a few euros a month).

Then there is the rental upside. When the box is idle, you can rent out its spare capacity, for example on Vast.ai. At even $0.20 an hour for 12 hours a day, that adds around $70 a month. It shortens the payback, or turns an idle machine into a small earner.

These are rough, illustrative figures. Your real numbers depend on your models, your usage, electricity prices, and (for rental) your hardware and current demand.

Why local AI

For regulated and privacy-sensitive work like law, finance, healthcare and the public sector, sending data to a foreign cloud is often a non-starter. Local AI gives you the productivity of modern models without the exposure. Your information never leaves your control, you are not locked into a provider's pricing, and there is no black box you depend on.

And if you simply value your freedom: local AI is yours. You get full ownership of the hardware, the models and the data. You get real privacy, because nothing leaves your control. And you get the most cost-efficient AI you can run, because there is no meter and no middleman. Your AI answers to you, not to a provider's terms of service, rate limits or content rules.
